1. Overview

Maket Lakay is a multi-vendor e-commerce marketplace connecting independent sellers — based in Haiti and across the Haitian diaspora — with customers through a single storefront. The platform handles checkout, order management, delivery coordination, and customer support centrally, while each vendor manages their own products and store.

Three roles drive the platform: customers who browse and buy, vendors who list and fulfill products, and admin/support staff who approve vendors, moderate content, and resolve issues. A fourth actor, Stripe, handles identity verification and payment settlement for real card transactions.

2. Customer Flow

2.1 Discovery & Browsing

1. Customer lands on the homepage — trending, new arrival, recommended, and deal sections pull from the live product catalog.
2. Browses by category, searches by keyword, or visits a specific vendor's store page.
3. Views a product detail page: description, price, stock, reviews, related products, and the selling store's profile.

2.2 Account & Sign-up

1. Sign-up requires only name, email, and password. A profiles row (role: customer by default) is created automatically.
2. Signed-in customers get a persistent account area: saved addresses, wishlist, order history, and submitted reviews.

2.4 Post-purchase

1. Order confirmation page, then live order tracking with a status timeline (pending → confirmed → processing → ready for delivery → out for delivery → delivered).
2. Customer can message support, open a dispute, or request a refund tied to a specific order.
3. After delivery, customer can leave a product review and a store review.

3. Vendor Flow

3.1 Becoming a Vendor

1. A signed-in customer applies at /sell — a multi-step form (seller identity, legal/ID details, store profile, payout preferences).
2. The application is reviewed by an admin at /admin/vendors.
3. On approval, a real vendor account and store are created automatically, and the applicant's role is promoted to vendor.

5. Payment Flow (Stripe Connect)

Because Maket Lakay is a marketplace — one checkout can pay several independent vendors — card payments are processed through Stripe Connect rather than a standard merchant account. Each vendor is individually onboarded and paid directly.

5.1 Vendor Payout Onboarding

Vendor opens Store Settings → picks their country → clicks "Connect with Stripe" → completes Stripe's own hosted verification form (identity, bank account) → redirected back to Maket Lakay → status syncs to "Active"

Maket Lakay never sees or stores the vendor's identity documents or bank details — that data goes directly to Stripe. Currently, onboarding requires a business or bank account in a country Stripe supports directly (e.g. the United States, Canada, France) — not Haiti. In practice this means diaspora-based vendors can complete payout setup today; support for Haiti-based vendors depends on Stripe expanding country coverage.

5.2 Checkout Gating

"Card" only appears as a selectable payment method when every vendor represented in the cart has completed Stripe Connect onboarding. If even one seller hasn't finished setup, Card is shown disabled with an explanation, and the customer can still complete checkout with another payment method.

5.3 Charge & Payout Split

Maket Lakay uses the Separate Charges and Transfers pattern: the customer's card is charged once, for the full order total, into the platform's own Stripe balance. Immediately afterward, the platform transfers each vendor's share (order subtotal minus the marketplace commission) directly to that vendor's own connected Stripe account.

Customer confirms card payment → Maket Lakay's server re-verifies the payment with Stripe → the order is recorded → a transfer is sent to each vendor's Stripe account for their share → order confirmation shown to the customer

The order is recorded before any transfer is attempted, so if a transfer fails for any reason, the customer's order is never lost — it's simply flagged for admin follow-up in the Payout Reconciliation view (Section 4).

5.4 What Stays Simulated

MonCash, NatCash, Bank Transfer, and the declined-test payment option remain frontend simulations by design — no real money moves through them regardless of Stripe's configuration status.

6. Roles & Access

RoleAccess
CustomerMarketplace, cart, checkout, account, orders, tracking, support.
VendorEverything under /vendor — scoped to their own store(s).
SupportShares /admin/support, /admin/orders, /admin/vendors, /admin/products with admin.
AdminFull platform oversight — everything above plus approvals, commissions, delivery-zone configuration, and reporting.

New sign-ups default to customer. Vendor access is granted only through the real application-and-approval flow. Admin/support access is granted by an existing admin promoting a user from /admin/users.
