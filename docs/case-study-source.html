The Opportunity

The gap

Haitian merchants — from artisans and food producers to small retailers — largely lack an accessible, trustworthy digital storefront tailored to their market. General-purpose global marketplaces are not built around Haiti-specific realities: local delivery logistics, regional payment methods (MonCash, NatCash) alongside international ones, bilingual/trilingual commerce (Kreyòl, French, English), and the strong economic bridge between Haiti and its diaspora communities abroad.

The bridge

The Haitian diaspora — concentrated in the United States, Canada, and France — maintains deep, active economic ties to Haiti, regularly sending goods, gifts, and support home. A marketplace that lets diaspora buyers purchase directly from Haitian vendors, with delivery handled locally, turns an informal, ad-hoc pattern into a structured commercial channel — for everyday goods, not just remittances.

Why now

  • Mobile-first internet access continues to expand across Haiti, growing the addressable base of both shoppers and small merchants who can participate in digital commerce.
  • Local payment rails (MonCash, NatCash) are already in wide everyday use, lowering the trust barrier for local transactions.
  • No dominant, trusted, Haiti-specific multi-vendor marketplace currently exists — the category is open.

Technical Foundation

The engineering choice that matters most for a marketplace: every workflow above is backed by a real, secured database — not a demo.

Modern, proven stack

Next.js (React) frontend, TypeScript throughout, Supabase (Postgres) for the database and authentication, Stripe for card payments. All widely-adopted, well-supported technology — no exotic dependencies that create long-term risk.

Security by default

Row Level Security is enabled on all 24 database tables. A customer can only ever see their own data; a vendor can only see and act on their own store's data; admin/support access is explicitly scoped by role. This is enforced at the database layer, not just in application code.

Built for real operations, not a demo

Every feature listed on the previous page was verified end-to-end against the live database with real accounts before being considered done — not just checked to "look right" in the UI.

Deployed & continuously delivered

The application auto-deploys to production on every merge, with type-checking, linting, and a full production build required to pass before any change ships.

Engineering velocity

The backend — spanning identity, catalog, orders, payments, delivery operations, vendor commerce, and the full support/dispute lifecycle — was designed, built, secured, and verified in roughly two weeks of focused, disciplined engineering. This pace, combined with the verification discipline described above, is a meaningful signal of execution capability for whoever takes this platform to launch and beyond.
